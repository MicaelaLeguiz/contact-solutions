# KPI Dictionary

## Purpose

This document defines the business metrics used throughout the project. Each KPI includes its business definition, calculation logic, analytical level, business objective, and the fact and dimension tables required for its calculation. The purpose of this document is to ensure consistent KPI definitions across the ETL process, data model, and Power BI dashboards

---

| KPI | Business Definition | Formula | Fact Table(s) | Dimension(s) | Level | Goal |
|------|---------------------|----------|---------------|--------------|-------|------|
| Conversion Rate | Percentage of effective customer contacts that result in a confirmed sale | Confirmed Sales / Effective Contacts | FactCalls, FactSales | DimDate, DimCustomer, DimCampaign, DimAgent | Strategic | Maximize |
| Profitability | Percentage of collected revenue that remains as profit after operating costs | (Paid Revenue − Operating Costs) / Paid Revenue | FactSales, FactCosts | DimDate | Strategic | Maximize |
| Expected Profit | Projected profit for a workforce configuration, weighted by the expected conversion rate | (Conversion Rate × Margin per Sale × Projected Contacts) − Fixed Costs | FactCalls, FactSales, FactCosts | DimDate, DimCampaign, DimAgent | Strategic | Maximize |
| Cost per Sale | Average operating cost incurred for each confirmed sale | Total Operating Cost / Confirmed Sales | FactSales, FactCosts | DimDate, DimCampaign | Strategic | Minimize |
| Contacts per Agent | Average number of customer contacts handled by each agent during a given period | Handled Contacts / Number of Agents | FactCalls | DimDate, DimAgent | Operational | Maximize |
| Sales per Agent | Average number of confirmed sales generated by each agent | Confirmed Sales / Number of Agents | FactCalls, FactSales | DimDate, DimAgent | Operational | Maximize |
| Average Handling Time (AHT) | Average time required to complete a customer interaction, including conversation, hold time, and after-call work | Σ (ACD Time + Hold Time + ACW Time) / Calls | FactCalls | DimDate, DimAgent, DimCampaign | Operational | Optimize |
| Productivity per Hour | Average number of customer contacts handled per hour worked | Handled Contacts / Worked Hours | FactCalls | DimDate, DimAgent | Operational | Maximize |
| Agent Utilization | Percentage of an agent's logged time spent handling customer interactions | (ACD Time + Hold Time + ACW Time) / Logged Time | FactCalls | DimDate, DimAgent | Operational | Maximize (within a sustainable range) |
| Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) | Average post-interaction customer satisfaction score | Average CSAT Score | FactCalls | DimDate, DimCampaign, DimAgent | Quality | ≥ 85% |
| Abandonment Rate | Percentage of customer interactions abandoned before completion | Abandoned Contacts / Total Contacts | FactCalls | DimDate, DimCampaign | Quality / Operational | Minimize |

---

## Notes

- **Confirmed Sales** include only completed sales recorded in **FactSales**
- **Paid Revenue** is calculated using the `paid_amount` field to reflect actual collected revenue rather than contracted sales
- **Handled Contacts** refer to customer interactions successfully managed by an agent
- **Effective Contacts** are customer interactions where meaningful communication was established (excluding unanswered calls, invalid numbers, and similar outcomes)
- **Logged Time** represents the total time an agent is available for work, including talk time, hold time, after-call work, and idle time
- **Expected Profit** is a simulated KPI used to evaluate different human–AI workforce configurations under budget and service quality constraints
